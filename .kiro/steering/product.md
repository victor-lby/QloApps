# QloApps Product Overview

QloApps is an open-source hotel reservation system and booking engine built on PHP. It's designed to help hotels of all sizes launch their booking websites and manage reservations.

## Key Features
- Hotel booking and reservation management
- Multi-language and multi-currency support
- Room management and availability tracking
- Customer management and guest services
- Payment processing integration
- Admin dashboard for hotel operations
- Modular architecture with plugin system

## Target Users
- Independent hotels
- Hotel chains
- Property managers
- Hospitality businesses

## Business Model
- Open source (OSL-3.0 license)
- Free core platform
- Commercial modules and support available
- Developed by Webkul

## Architecture
Built on a modified PrestaShop e-commerce framework, adapted specifically for hotel and hospitality management.
